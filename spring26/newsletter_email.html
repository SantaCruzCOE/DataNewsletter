OneSource Customizations

Tailoring student-level data to meet the needs of educators

Our integrated student data system, OneSource, continues to improve and expand. Over the Spring, we finished the onboarding and ETL process for our newest district, Santa Cruz City Schools. We also worked closely with San Lorenzo Valley teachers and other stakeholders to deliver several key dashboard enhancements and new features, including:

  • • New Assessment Charts in the OneSource Overview Page: Added charts for a quick overview of results from any of the available benchmark assessments for the district/county, such as I-Ready, NWEA, STAR, etc.
  • • New Comprehensive Table of Student Data for Teachers: After meeting with teachers at San Lorenzo Valley Middle School to understand their needs, we created a new comprehensive table of student data for the teachers' overview page. We also rearranged the existing visualizations in the overview page as requested by teachers and added new charts that teachers thought would be useful to have easily accessible in the overview page.
  • • Attendance & Program Conformance: Standardized absence codes and conformed program groups across districts for cleaner, countywide reporting.
  • • Program Category Group Filter: Added a filter by program category group to allow users to filter by program category more easily.

OneSource is currently available for the following districts: San Lorenzo Valley, Santa Cruz City Schools, Pajaro Valley, and Santa Cruz COE. If your district is interested in OneSource, please let us know!

🚀 App Development

Google Forms add-on, new business archive reports app, and vibe coding support

Form Friend Installer

We have developed a Google Forms add-on that enables you to automate sending calendar event invitations to your form respondents. You can send different calendar invitations based on the answers they provide in the form, or simply send one calendar invitation to all respondents. Install form friend and give it a try!

Note: At the moment the Form Friend Installer add-on is only available for use within Santa Cruz COE domain. If anyone at a district would like to use it, please contact us and we can work on getting it set up for your domain.

Business Archive Reports

We have successfully migrated the legacy business archive reports application (CECC Archive) and its datasets to a modern stack running on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). The new cloud-native architecture delivers significant improvements in performance, security, and usability. This new application is live and currently open to a selected group of admin users in the COE business department. They will test it and open it as needed to the corresponding district stakeholders.

Vibe Coding Support Group

Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools that enable people to create applications with minimal or no coding experience (vibe coding) are evolving rapidly, and our brilliant staff at the COE is noticing. This Spring, we noticed a significant increase in the interest from staff across the COE and school districts to use these tools. This led to the creation of a new Vibe Coding Support group with the purpose of evaluating how we can best support our staff in the responsible use of these tools while minimizing the risks of exposing sensitive data and other vulnerabilities to our systems and networks. If you want to know more about this group and the guidelines that we are developing, feel free to reach out to anyone on the Tech+ team, or wait to hear more from us this summer/fall.

⚙️ Automations and Integrations

Business process automations for events scheduling and secure document sharing

COE Events Management Tool

We built a custom events management tool on top of a Google Sheet to support scheduling and tracking of events across the COE and school districts. Developed in collaboration with Marcia Russell and the Curriculum and Instruction team, the system includes:

  • • A UI interface to create new events and write or update them into the Google Sheet.
  • • An AI assistant to propose the best days to schedule your event based on conflicts and preferences.
  • • The generation of a Google Calendar including all of the events from the Google Sheet.

Outdoor Science School Digital Migration

We helped the Santa Cruz COE Outdoor Science School in their transition to a digital registration system built in Google Forms, Sheets, and Google Apps Script. The system automates the creation of the required Registration and Health documents and places them into a Google Drive folder for the respective school site.

File Share Audit Tool

Phishing attempts often leverage external document sharing to target staff, making detection difficult. Unfortunately, Google Workspace's admin panel does not provide a robust audit system for external document sharing, so we developed a custom document audit and alert system. Our new tool provides a monitoring and alerting system that scans external document sharing and sends notifications when an external file has been shared with a threshold of users within the organization. We are also exploring automated mitigation strategies to block suspicious file sharing.

🏫 Student Information Systems

Student Information Systems (SIS) support for the COE's Career Advancement Charter (CAC)

This Spring we continued supporting the great effort of the COE's CAC in improving and optimizing their PowerSchool SIS implementation and the processes and workflows around it.
The goal is to make it easier for CAC administrators and teachers to keep accurate records for attendance, grades and other student data required for compliance and reporting purposes. In addition, the new CAC implementation will keep student data more private and secure.
