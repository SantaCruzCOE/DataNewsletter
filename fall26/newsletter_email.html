Quarterly Update: Data Analytics and Development Department
11/24/26

Dear Santa Cruz COE Community,

We are pleased to share our Fall 2026 quarterly update from the Data Analytics and Development team.

Through the summer and fall months, our team has been dedicated to advancing our integrated data system, implementing new administrative automations, and supporting the COE departments and Santa Cruz County districts with their data and app development needs. Below are key highlights of the progress achieved this fall.

For more details, please visit the Data Portal and Educational Data page on the Santa Cruz COE website.

📊 Data Portal Updates

State data updates and new Reclassified Fluent English Proficient (RFEP) dashboard

Fall is a time of multiple new data releases by the California Department of Education (CDE). We have updated the Santa Cruz COE Data Portal to provide up-to-date information for county educators and the public:

• Updated State Assessment Visualizations: Integrated the latest annual data for CAASPP and CAST data visualizations. We are also working on a new set of visualizations that will include ELPAC results and should be available in the coming weeks.
• Other updated visualizations:
– Chronic Absenteeism Rates (2017–2026)
– Suspension Rates (2015–2026)
– Expulsion Rates (2012–2026)
– 4-Year Cohort High School Graduation Rates (2017–2026)
– Cumulative Enrollment by District & Student Groups (2017–2026)
– English Learner Progress Indicator (ELPI) Current Status (2019–2026)
– Teaching Assignment Monitoring Outcomes (AMO) Report
• New RFEP Reclassification Dashboard: Added an in-depth dashboard tracking Reclassified Fluent English Proficient (RFEP) student progress across districts, showcasing longitudinal reclassification trends, counts, and language acquisition milestones.

Schoolytics Pilot for OneSource

Empowering educators with an intuitive, role-based integrated analytics platform

This fall, the Data Analytics team kicked off a pilot of Schoolytics with participating school districts across Santa Cruz County. The pilot aims to evaluate a new analytics platform for our integrated analytics initiative, OneSource.

• Role-Based User Experiences: Developing specialized dashboard views tailored to distinct roles—giving classroom teachers quick access to formative indicators, while equipping counselors and administrators with whole-student views.
• Collaborative Evaluation: Hosting structured feedback sessions with district stakeholders to establish key evaluation criteria, refine visual reporting, and ensure the platform directly addresses educators' highest-priority data questions.

The pilot is currently underway, with comprehensive feedback collection planned for the remainder of the fall semester. Findings from the pilot will inform whether we switch to Schoolytics as our analytics platform supporting OneSource. If approved, we will begin the full implementation at the beginning of the 2026-27 school year.

⚙️ PowerSchool Automations

Streamlined Google account provisioning and SIS workflows

Automating repetitive administrative tasks within our Student Information Systems (SIS) remains a top priority. Over the fall, we rolled out several high-impact automations:

• Automated Student Google Account Creation: Launched a new Google account provisioning application tool (studentaccounts.santacruzcoe.org). The system automatically creates Google Workspace accounts for incoming students in Alternative Education and Special Education programs based on the enrollment records in their PowerSchool SIS. It also provides a management tool for daily student account maintenance workflows and two-way syncing between PowerSchool and the Google Admin Console.
• District-of-Residence (DOR) Automation: Built an automated address verification and district boundary lookup tool utilizing mapping APIs, streamlining residency determination and eliminating manual address validation during enrollment.
• PowerSchool SIS Automations: Continued developing our PowerSchool companion tools and custom plugins to automate attendance tracking synchronization, status code updates, and streamlined data handoffs.

🏫 CAC and Alternative Education Support

Modernizing student onboarding, attendance, and compliance workflows

We deepened our operational partnership with the Career Advancement Charter (CAC) and COE Alternative Education programs to enhance data workflows and reporting accuracy:

• CAC Digital Intake & Onboarding: Designed and deployed end-to-end registration workflows that automatically route student intake responses directly into centralized tracking systems, including timestamped contract tracking and teacher orientation guides.
• AltEd Attendance & Health Support: Enhanced monitoring and reporting tools for student attendance, chronic absenteeism, and student health records—including assisting school nursing staff with automated immunization compliance reporting directly from the SIS.
• SIS Transition Planning: Began initial transition planning for updated PowerSchool configurations across Alternative Education and Special Education to improve student data privacy and system usability.

🤝 Support to Departments and School Districts

Collaborative solutions across Santa Cruz County educational programs

Beyond core analytics, our team provided specialized technical and data support to multiple departments, schools, and partner agencies:

• Santa Cruz City Schools (SCCS): Supported the onboarding of SCCS into OneSource, including user training, data connections, and data validation.
• Live Oak School District: Built custom interactive dashboards and drill-down visualization reports in Looker Studio to support district leadership in monitoring key student progress indicators.
• Special Education (SPED): Enhanced student health page mappings, supported specialized enrollment requests, and assisted with annual state reporting preparation.
• Early Childhood Programs: Supported Hubbe data system implementations across regional early education partners (including YMCA, Bridges to Kinder, and Coastal Community) and assisted with parent survey analysis for the Early Childcare Program.
• Curriculum & Instruction: Delivered feature enhancements to the COE Events Management Tool and automated Google Group synchronization to streamline professional development coordination.
• Human Resources & Departmental Automations: Developed automated folder creation and progress tracking systems for the Certificated Employee Evaluation cycle, and streamlined vendor order form workflows for Alt Ed curriculum procurement.
• North Santa Cruz County SELPA: Supported the automation of the Low Incidence equipment request workflow, converting web intake into auto-merged documentation packages with approval routing, and provided other technical support and maintenance for SELPA reporting tools.
• CalKIDS Verification for Multiple Districts: Supported multiple districts with the verification of student eligibility for CalKIDS through the development, maintenance, and user support of a tool that automates CalKIDS verifications based on the districts' provided lists of State Student IDs. The goal is to ensure students across Santa Cruz County access their California Kids Investment and Development Savings accounts.
• COE-Wide Strategic AI Leadership Team: Participated in the development of AI policies and guidelines for the county office of education. Also created a dashboard to aid in analyzing the Santa Cruz COE AI staff survey regarding AI adoption and perception.
• Vibe Coding Support for Multiple Departments: Conducted data privacy and security reviews for vibe-coded apps, including the countywide Behavioral Health Resource Directory, the SCCS Youth Substance Use Screener, and an automated document redaction tool developed by the Superintendent's department to comply with the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).
